# About me

I'm Önder Samet, the person behind töre.

I think of myself as a storyteller: I turn observable processes into systems. I studied Communication and Design at Bilkent University, and töre is where those two habits meet, noticing how thinking actually happens and building something that keeps it from getting lost.

## Why I am building töre

I do a lot of my thinking in conversations with AI tools. Those conversations produce decisions, reasons and open questions, and then they scroll away. Coming back to a topic weeks later, I kept asking the same thing:

> What did I last think about this, which decision still stands, what changed, which question is open, and where can I continue?

töre is my attempt to answer that question for myself first, with my own conversations, and then for anyone else who works the same way.

## What I hold to

- **My words, the source and the AI's interpretation stay separate.** An AI suggestion never becomes my view on its own.
- **Silence is not consent.** A question closes only when the person closes it.
- **Attention is something to protect, not to take.** töre has no notifications, streaks or feeds designed to pull you back.
- **Your thinking stays on your computer** unless you decide to send it somewhere.

## How töre is made

töre is an independent project. I build it with AI tools and say so openly; I lead the product direction, the user experience and the feature decisions, and I review what gets delivered.

There is no company, team, revenue or customer behind it yet. It is a preview that I use myself.
